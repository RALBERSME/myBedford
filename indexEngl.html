No Roses Bloom in the Diva’s Shadow
(Book Genre: Romantic Suspense)


Bedford, 1953: For young seamstress Liz, life is a constant balancing act on the silken thread her mother spins. Eyleen—a former musical star whose glory has long since faded—tolerates no other queen beside her. While her brother Edgar is showered with love, Liz is left with the role of scapegoat — and the grueling work at the sewing machine that keeps the family afloat.

When Clark, a charming yet unconventional author, enters Liz’s life, hope for her own happiness begins to blossom for the first time.

But a dark secret hangs over the family’s past. The laboriously maintained web of lies collapses when a mysterious accident occurs.

Trapped between a toxic mother’s pathological jealousy and the bloody traces of an unfathomable crime, Liz must make a choice:
Will she remain a mere extra in her family’s destructive drama—or find the courage to write her own life story?

A profound psychological novel about toxic family ties, the paralyzing weight of guilt, and the arduous path to freedom.
